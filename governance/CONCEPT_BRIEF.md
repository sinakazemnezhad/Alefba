## Thesis

Most “Persian AI” is an English-born model wearing a Farsi skin.

**Alefbâ** is the opposite: a foundation language system whose first world is Persian — the alphabet, orthography, lexicon, stories, and books — so reasoning and generation emerge from Persian itself.

The design is an **ontogeny of understanding**: pure code → algorithm (law of reading) → letter → word → story → book → meaning → literary consciousness → lifelong growth — **all soil is Persian literature**. “Consciousness” means semantic presence inside that literary world, not a claim of biological AGI.

## What it is

A commercial–scientific program to train, evaluate, and ship a **Persian-native foundation model** and product stack:

1. **Corpus science** — licensed and curated Persian text from alphabet primers through classical and contemporary literature
2. **Tokenizer + script fidelity** — Persian alphabet, diacritics, ZWNJ, RTL, morphology-aware segmentation
3. **Pretrain → instruct → align** — stages measured on Persian benchmarks, not English proxies alone
4. **Products** — API, enterprise assistant, education, and publisher tooling

## What it is not

- A translation layer on top of an English LLM
- A chatbot wrapper with no corpus law
- A claim of “full AGI” without evaluation receipts
