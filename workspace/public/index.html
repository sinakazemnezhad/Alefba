Scientific edge

Investors fund science that compounds.

Math scores guesses. Persian books turn those guesses into language. Stories and long works turn language into meaning. Gates and months turn meaning into a system that gets smarter on a timeline.

Model stack

  • Persian-aware tokenizer with alphabet coverage guarantees
  • Base decoder LLM sized to compute reality, then scaled on measured lift
  • SFT on human Persian pairs, then DPO/ORPO with native raters
  • Optional RAG over licensed books for grounded citation products
  • Eval harness: orthography probes, FA reading suites, human rubrics — dated before scale
  • Baselines: named open checkpoints on the same Persian splits — not English proxies alone

Evaluation law

  • Orthography and morphology probes
  • Persian reading comprehension and knowledge suites
  • Human rubrics for fluency, register, cultural fit
  • No capability claim without dated score cards

Corpus law

  • Licensed, publisher-partnered, and public-domain first
  • Provenance, license class, and quality grade per shard
  • Hard train / validation / public-eval separation
  • Volume claimed in bands — never as “all books”

How it gets smarter

  1. Math loop — predict next Persian piece → measure error → update weights
  2. Language — alphabet, words, habits from Persian literature
  3. Meaning — stories + books teach causality, register, metaphor
  4. Timeline — M3→M8→M12→M18 gates prove real Persian lift each season

Four surfaces that Persianize the training loop

Every large model runs the same engine: predict the next token, measure error, update weights. Alefbâ keeps the Latin factory — Python, CUDA, standard pretrain recipes. What we retune is every place Persian enters.

  • Entry — a Persian tokenizer for ا ب پ and joining forms
  • Feed — staged curriculum: alphabet, stories, licensed books
  • Target — weights learn the next Persian piece
  • Proof — native raters and dated score cards before scale

FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Invest vs donate — what is the difference?

Invest is an equity conversation. Donate is a gift that fuels public research and open eval cards — not shares.

Where does the training data come from?

Licensed publisher deals, public-domain text, and partnered corpora. Scraping is not the strategy. Every shard needs provenance and license class.

What does GPU money buy?

Training and eval runs for tokenizer experiments, base pretrain, and instruct alignment — tracked against score cards.

Who owns the model?

Company-owned foundation weights for commercial API/Studio. Selective research checkpoints may be released; donors do not receive equity by default.

How does participation work?

Pick a first action (corpus, eval guild, builder, ambassador). We route you into the matching crew lane after interest capture.

What is the timeline?

Eighteen months from foundation inventory to vertical pilots — capital and corpus speed change the calendar.

What baselines will receipts compare against?

Named open checkpoints on the same Persian splits — orthography, morphology, reading comprehension, human rubrics. No English-translated proxy as the center.

Why cite Chinese models?

GLM and similar systems showed that native corpus, tokenizer, and evaluation matter on shared hardware. We honor that precedent and apply the same scientific pattern for Persian literature.
